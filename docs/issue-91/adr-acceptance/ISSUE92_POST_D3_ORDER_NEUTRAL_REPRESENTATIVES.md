**Status:** Central architecture/implementation contract, **not** a qualified implementation or permission for a D4 run.  
**Authority:** [ADR-91-2026-11-09](../ADR-91-2026-11-09-GRAPHITI-OPENVIKING-MEMORY-ARCHITECTURE.md), Issue #92; immutable [D3 evidence](../evidence/92-adr-d3-recalibrated/README.md) @ `944f720e60d4071e95b096f851c6c5bbbfeb1e68`.

**Historical results:** untouched prospective, D1, D2 and D3 scorecards remain **failures** for useful-fact retention; no new acceptance is claimed.

## D3 decision and diagnosis

- D3's matched two-stage previous arm persisted 0/16 independently valuable targets. Corrected persisted **2/16**, including Sphinx header-row support and Pylint verbose-argument incompatibility, but **14/16** were still missed. It persisted **2/11 nonvaluable duplicate-family representatives** and no generic boilerplate. Each of four corrected remote episodes had four-surface receipts. The installed `development-knowledge` group and historical groups were unchanged by the campaign (host-reported and published).
- The source-level order dependence is real: `reviewProposedFacts()` obtains prior same-group statements from `ReviewStore.list()` in `approved`, `queued`, `submission_unknown`, `persisted` **and `deferred`** states, and `runAdmitted()` reviews clusters sequentially and promotes approved facts after that portion of work. Thus a previously considered or earlier-persisted sibling can bias a later reviewer towards `repetition_candidate`. In particular a **deferred, never persisted** statement is presently supplied as an apparent prior fact.
- The D3 host review attributes the displacement of certain valuable Sphinx/Django siblings to earlier nonvaluable family representatives (`sp3`, `dj5`). That accounts for some losses, **not** every missing target. The published valuable target first-loss stage is 11 REVIEW_DEFER, 3 DISCOVERED_OTHER_FACT, 2 retained. The reported 7 repetition / 6 not_substantive / 1 review_uncertain are reviewer-level labels, not necessarily mutually exclusive target-level categories.
- `promoteOne()` already enforces exact normalized local/remote duplicate checks; these **are not** semantic-family representative selection and must not be removed to force improvement. A model declaring two claims synonymous is not sufficient original-source evidence that one safely subsumes the other.

## Governing next implementation invariants

1. **Source first, independent support first.** Keep the high-recall original-source discovery and bounded exact-span grounding. Independently evaluate every potentially useful atomic candidate for (a) quoted passage supporting a specific development predicate, (b) appropriate reported/verified evidence class and temporal status, and (c) future coding usefulness. A generic phrase or unsubstantiated boilerplate may not be made useful by nearby source text or a model gloss.
2. **Decouple usefulness from novelty.** A fact can be useful even if a semantic neighbor already exists. `repetition_candidate` is not a hard suppression criterion until both members have been compared for equivalence and representative quality. Never let an unverified, deferred or low-information sibling become an authoritative graph-memory anchor.
3. **Order-neutral prospective siblings.** For an explicitly bounded finite post-exit qualification window, gather and source-validate the candidate pool BEFORE automatic Graphiti promotion. Evaluate utility independently, then compare likely same-meaning candidates jointly and select the most source-supported *substantive* representative. Stable candidate IDs/source refs and a canonical ordering must make the result insensitive to queue order and cluster arrival order. Do not use a positional first-match/keyword-similarity proxy as semantic equivalence proof.
4. **Pre-existing graph facts and mutations.** Preserve already terminal persisted episodes, operator CAS, source proof, positive remote identity and no-replay. An already persisted fact can inform comparison, but a better new candidate must not silently replace/delete/supersede it; operator review is required for such a proposed upgrade. Automatic contradiction/supersession, new ontology, new service and broader installed behavior remain out of scope.
5. **Uncertain equivalence/representative quality.** Keep competing source-supported candidates reviewable with exact source and reason rather than silently SKIPping. Prefer a modest number of safe review/alternate candidates to false-negative loss of independently valuable technical knowledge; however, do not automatically persist multiple claims merely to inflate retained-target counts.
6. **Crash, time and resource bounds.** The finite candidate cohort, independent review decisions and representative arbitration must have durable local identity before *any* remote write intent. Interrupted/ambiguous model calls and Graphiti submissions remain nonreplayable without the existing authenticated recovery path. Do not weaken EX/quiet-grace, bounded queue/cluster/episode limits, privacy/group/workspace/temporal gates or stable one-fact-per-episode UUID/reconciliation. The normal one-pass production path stays default.
7. **Other false negatives still matter.** Representative arbitration alone cannot fix the 3 DISCOVERED_OTHER_FACT valuable targets or the nonrepetition `not_substantive` false negatives. Include a bounded, independently supported corrective path for candidate passage selection when the quoted span omits the source's actual technical predicate. No automatic cross-excerpt synthesis, unsupported normalized claim or fabricated fix.

## Central-owned implementation boundary

Prefer a cohesive private, opt-in **finite-batch curation plan** that freezes source-validated candidate snapshots, independent review/utility outcomes and semantic-family comparisons before materializing approved ReviewStore records or remote writes. The development source is currently incremental; adapting its persistence boundary must be designed together with durable plan recovery, queue ownership and existing reviewed/unknown writes, rather than scattering prompt-only exceptions into the live worker. Do not extend the interactive hooks, require a new backend, or introduce a generic graph ontology.

The normal production one-pass code stays untouched until the new finite-batch path independently qualifies. Implement order-neutral representative selection with its own source-anchored tests; no local Build agent should invent the core algorithm under a mechanical-integration label. A mere reordering of source IDs is insufficient to prove better representative quality. Historic `ReviewStore` records and H2/H3/H4/D1/D2/D3 scorecards remain immutable.

## Required Central regression coverage before another model campaign

- **Order invariance:** permute the same source-authenticated 24-cluster candidate set and its same-family Sphinx/Django siblings. Selected representative identities and promotion decisions must not depend on arrival order, original cluster order or review/remote write order.
- **Representative quality:** a technical predicate stated by a specific original source must win over generic/repeated/boilerplate variants; unrelated constraints sharing a project/component must never be collapsed into one family. When the comparative judgment is uncertain, defer with provenance and operator option rather than inventing a winner.
- **Coverage:** all 16 valuable frozen targets can be individually mapped to model-visible original sources, viable candidate passages, independent utility judgments and terminal status; no model-facing counter is treated as proof of semantic discovery. Retention improvement must be measured **alongside** low-value/redundant persistence.
- **Safety:** privacy, source class, temporal uncertainty, exact offsets, multi-fact review/UUID identities, Graphiti group/source index, operator CAS and unknown-submission no-replay remain intact. No unreviewed normalized claims in automatic graph output.
- **Crash faults:** interrupt before/after batch freeze, between review/arbitration, before first remote intent and after acknowledged/unknown writes; demonstrate the correct durable state and never silently replay a possibly completed call.
- **Actual utility:** expose-case qualification can use the now-exposed frozen D3 corpus; it does not qualify the new source. Only after an independently reviewed exposed-case recovery should one new untouched prospective acceptance cohort be frozen before model output.
